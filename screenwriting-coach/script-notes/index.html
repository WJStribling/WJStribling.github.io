Coaching / Script Notes

Feature Screenplay Notes and Script Consulting

You send me your feature. I read it closely, write detailed notes, and then we spend 90 minutes on a call working through them together, so you leave knowing exactly what to do in the next draft.

This is a consultant's read rather than ongoing coaching: one draft, one thorough round of feedback. It works well when you have a complete draft and want an experienced, outside eye before a rewrite, a contest deadline, or sending the script to producers and reps.

What you get

Written notes covering what is working and what is not: structure, character, scene construction, dialogue, and whether the script delivers on its premise. Then a 90-minute conversation where we dig into the notes, answer your questions, and plan the rewrite. My goal is to help you reach your vision for the script, not to impose mine.

Who reads your script

I have taught feature screenwriting at Chapman University's Dodge College since 2019, at both the undergraduate and graduate levels. I hold an MFA in screenwriting from Chapman, where my feature screenplay won the Cecil Award, and a BFA from NYU Tisch. As a director, my features include Bear with Us and The Mirror Game, and I co-wrote the video game Ledgerbound. I read your script as a teacher and as someone who has to turn pages into a film.

How it works

Use the form below to tell me about your script. We confirm timing, you send the PDF, and we schedule the session once the notes are ready. Rates assume a feature screenplay of standard length; longer scripts, rush turnarounds and multi-draft work are by arrangement. If you would rather keep working together after the notes, ongoing coaching picks up from there.
