One-on-One Screenwriting Coaching

Ongoing, private coaching for writers at every stage, from a first-time writer who wants a crash course to a professional polishing a final draft. We work through your script together at whatever pace the project needs.

Feature screenplays are my specialty and the subject of the courses I teach at Chapman University's Dodge College. My private curriculum is adapted from those undergraduate and graduate courses, so you get the same tools my film school students use, applied to your project.

What a session covers

Each session is sixty minutes, built around where you are. That might be developing an idea, building a protagonist, breaking story and outlining, reviewing pages in progress, or planning a rewrite. Clients come away with tools and materials they keep using on their own.

Beyond the page

Sessions can also cover the business side: pitching and packaging, understanding markets and festivals, and how to put a finished script to work. The aim is to help you build both the craft and the career of a working screenwriter.

Who you are working with

I have taught screenwriting at Dodge College since 2019. I hold an MFA in screenwriting from Chapman, where my feature screenplay won the Cecil Award, and a BFA from NYU Tisch. I write and direct features, including Bear with Us and The Mirror Game, and I co-wrote the video game Ledgerbound. I coach writers in Los Angeles and remotely. If you just want one thorough round of feedback on a finished draft, see script notes.
