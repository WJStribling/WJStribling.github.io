Feature film

Bear with Us

A well-meaning control freak drags his girlfriend to a cabin in the woods to stage the perfect marriage proposal. A ravenous bear, an affable forest ranger, and a crazy woodsman have other plans.

Watch on Prime Video Apple TV Tubi, free

Trailer

Colin has the cabin, the ring and the speech. What he does not have is a plan for the bear.

Bear with Us is a comedy about how far people will go to save a relationship that may not be worth saving. Every attempt at the perfect proposal is interrupted, and the interruptions keep getting worse.

The film spent a year on the festival circuit winning awards before being released by Comedy Dynamics.
