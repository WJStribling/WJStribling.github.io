The Mirror Game

Two childhood friends find themselves in Vegas on the same weekend. One night together, and one life-altering favor, will change everything for them both.

Trailer

Rose asks Abe for a favor. It’s the kind of favor that reorders a friendship, and they have one night to decide what to do about it.

The Mirror Game is a two-hander carried by Teya Patt and Michael Tennant, from a screenplay by Marissa Flaxbart. It had its world premiere at Cinequest in 2022 and went on to play festivals across the country.
