Coaching

Screenwriting Coaching, Teaching & Script Consulting

As a private screenwriting coach, I offer personalized, one-on-one guidance for writers at every stage of their craft, from crash courses for beginners to polishing final drafts for professionals. Feature screenplays are my specialty, and the subject of the courses I teach at both the undergraduate and graduate levels.

I have taught screenwriting since 2019 at Chapman University's Dodge College, and I work with private clients alongside that teaching. My philosophy on screenwriting is always evolving and I love sharing my own experience as a writer with my students and clients.

One-on-one screenwriting coaching

We work through your script together, at whatever pace the project needs. I have adapted my private curriculum from the graduate and undergraduate courses I teach at Dodge College, a top U.S. film school.

Script notes and consulting

If you want a consultant's read rather than ongoing coaching, I also provide detailed script notes and one-off feedback sessions on a draft.

Industry strategy

Beyond creative development, I provide professional strategies for navigating the film and television industry, from pitching and packaging to understanding markets and festivals. Whether you are writing your first screenplay or refining a project for production, my goal is to help you build both the artistry and the career of a working screenwriter.
