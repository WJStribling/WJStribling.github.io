William J. Stribling

William J. Stribling is a director and screenwriter in Los Angeles.

His films include The Mirror Game (Cinequest 2022), Bear with Us, and the PBS concert film en la sala, and he is the primary music video director for Making Movies. His fourth feature, CHLOE!, is in pre-production. He teaches screenwriting at Chapman University.
