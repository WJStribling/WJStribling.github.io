Ledgerbound

An overworked insurance adjuster is the only one who can save the fantasy kingdom of Eldarra. First she has to get her paperwork in order.

Play on Steam

Trailer

Rayna processes claims for a living. Now the claims involve monsters, magic and a kingdom on the brink, and her coworkers are her only backup.

Ledgerbound is a comedic narrative tactical RPG. Between turn-based battles, the team regroups at camp, where the player manages their time, builds relationships and, within HR guidelines, romances their colleagues.

William co-wrote the game with his frequent collaborator Russ Nickel, lead writer of Helldivers 2. It won Excellence in Narrative at the 2026 DevGAMM Awards.
